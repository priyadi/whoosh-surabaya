Whoosh to Surabaya Calculator

What happens if the Jakarta–Bandung high-speed railway is extended to Surabaya, along one of the three alternatives in KCIC's study or another combination of the corridors on its map: travel time, speed profile, fares, a door-to-door comparison with the flights to Juanda from both Jakarta airports (HLP–SUB and CGK–SUB), the economics of building it, and CO₂. Choose the route and the stops, move the sliders; every number is derived live, with formulas and references inline.
